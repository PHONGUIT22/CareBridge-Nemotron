# CareBridge Ambient OS — Product Context

> **Living Product Specification**  
> *Designed for ambient smart displays, remote geriatric care, and clinical AI triage.*

---

## 1. Product Vision & Mission

**CareBridge Ambient OS** transforms ambient countertop and nightstand smart displays (such as the Amazon Echo Show 10, wall-mounted tablets, and bedside docks) into a proactive, dignified healthcare companion for independent seniors and their remote family caregivers.

### The Problem
- **Medication Non-Adherence & Confusion:** Polypharmacy among seniors age 70+ leads to missed doses, accidental double-dosing, and dangerous drug interactions. Small pill bottle labels and complex regimens create anxiety.
- **Disconnected Family Caregivers:** Adult children ("the sandwich generation") experience chronic worry when unable to verify if aging parents took morning or evening medications on time.
- **Fragmented Symptom Triage:** When seniors feel dizzy, nauseated, or uncomfortable after taking medications, they either ignore symptoms until a crisis occurs or panic and call emergency services unnecessarily.
- **Unfriendly Digital Interfaces:** Traditional healthcare apps require navigating complex smartphone menus, typing on small virtual keyboards, or remembering app passwords—failing elderly users with tremors, arthritis, or low vision.

### The Solution
An ambient, glanceable operating system operating 24/7 on a bedside or kitchen counter display:
1. **Glanceable from 6 Feet Away:** High-contrast, large-format clock, upcoming dose countdown, and status indicators visible across the room.
2. **Multimodal Voice-First Interaction:** Powered by Amazon Alexa voice commands and AWS Bedrock Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5 for natural speech recognition and calm clinical triage.
3. **One-Tap Dignity:** A massive, tremor-tolerant touch target (`"I TOOK MY PILL"`) with instant celebratory confetti and audio reinforcement.
4. **Remote Caregiver Telemetry:** Instant synchronization via Model Context Protocol (MCP) and SQLite event streaming to family dashboards.

## 2. Target User Personas

### Primary Persona: Eleanor (The Independent Senior)
- **Age:** 78 years old. Living independently at home.
- **Clinical Profile:** Mild hypertension, early-stage osteoarthritis in hands, mild age-related macular degeneration (requires large fonts and high contrast).
- **Medication Regimen:** 4 daily prescriptions (Amlodipine 5mg morning, Baby Aspirin 81mg noon, Metformin 500mg evening, Atorvastatin 20mg bedtime).
- **Device Placement:** Bedside nightstand, 3 to 5 feet from her pillow.
- **Needs & Habits:**
  - Needs to know *right now* if she already took her morning pill without opening an app or searching through bottles.
  - Wants hands-free confirmation when waking up ("Alexa, did I take my blood pressure medicine?").
  - Requires large, high-contrast touch buttons when her fingers are stiff in the morning.
  - Values dignity: wants to feel autonomous and in control of her life, not treated like an invalid.

### Secondary Persona: Sarah (The Remote Adult Caregiver)
- **Age:** 48 years old. Eleanor's daughter, working full-time 45 minutes away.
- **Needs & Habits:**
  - Needs real-time peace of mind that Eleanor took her morning pills by 09:30 AM without needing to call and nag her every day.
  - Reviews weekly adherence matrices and vital trend anomalies (e.g., blood pressure spikes).
  - Inspects AI triage logs and MCP tool execution when Eleanor reports feeling dizzy or unusual.

### Tertiary Persona: Dr. Reynolds (The Attending Clinician / Pharmacist)
- **Role:** Primary Care Physician reviewing patient adherence history during bi-monthly checkups.
- **Needs:**
  - Clear, structured adherence logs (taken vs. missed timestamps, dose notes, vital correlations).
  - Transparent, auditable AI clinical rationale generated by AWS Bedrock during symptom queries.

## 4. Core Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

### 1. The Morning Pill Routine
> *"When I wake up and look over at my nightstand, I want to see clearly whether I have pills to take right now, so that I never miss a dose or accidentally take it twice."*
- **Ambient State:** Senior Clock in high-contrast cyan neon, adherence progress bar (e.g., `1 / 4 Doses (25%)`), and an orange upcoming dose card.
- **Action:** Eleanor taps the huge `"I TOOK MY PILL"` button or says *"Alexa, I took my pill"*.
- **Confirmation:** Confetti burst, emerald taken state, audio feedback: *"Great job! I've marked your Amlodipine as taken."*

### 2. Voice-Activated Symptom & Interaction Triage
> *"When I feel dizzy or lightheaded after taking a new medication, I want to ask my nightstand device what to do, so that I get calm, safe medical advice without panic."*
- **Action:** Eleanor speaks: *"Alexa, I feel a little dizzy after taking my blood pressure pill."*
- **System Action:** Dispatches to `clinicalAdvisor` MCP tool -> Invokes AWS Bedrock Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5.
- **Result:**
  - Speaks reassuring audio immediately: *"Sit or lie down safely. Feeling lightheaded can happen with blood pressure medicine. I am displaying guidance on your screen."*
  - Renders `ClinicalAdviceCard` on display: Triage level badge (Moderate / Amber), plain-language action steps, hydration guidance, and caregiver alert notice.

### 3. Caregiver Oversight & Adherence Matrix
> *"When I'm at work during lunch, I want to review my mother's adherence punch-card and vitals, so that I can intervene early if she is skipping doses."*
- **Action:** Caregiver Hub tab displays 7-day adherence matrix, quick vitals (Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, Glucose), and dose log timestamps.

## 5. Voice & Tone Guidelines

The CareBridge Ambient OS voice (Alexa TTS and screen microcopy) adheres to four core pillars:

| Pillar | How We Sound | How We Never Sound |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reassuring & Calm** | *"Sit down comfortably and rest for 10 minutes. This is a known mild reaction."* | *"DANGER: Potential adverse drug event detected!"* |
| **Dignified & Adult** | *"Great job Eleanor, your morning Amlodipine is recorded."* | *"Good girl! Here's a star for taking your medicine!"* |
| **Clear & Concise** | *"Time for Metformin, 500 milligrams with food."* | *"Patient is scheduled for administration of biguanide antidiabetic agent."* |
| **Transparent & Trustworthy** | *"Assessed by Claude 4.5 Clinical Model. Sarah has been notified."* | Black-box magic or unreferenced AI claims. |

## 6. Success Metrics & Guardrails

1. **Adherence Accuracy:** Zero double-log discrepancies; immediate optimistic UI updates backed by persistent SQLite WAL storage.
2. **Clinical Safety Guardrail:** Bedrock prompts explicitly enforce standard medical triage protocols: always advise calling emergency services (911/000) if chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or facial drooping is mentioned.
3. **Glanceability SLA:** Any senior must be able to determine today's medication status within **2 seconds** of looking at the display.
4. **Latency Budget:** Voice intent routing to Bedrock response under **2.5 seconds**; local dose toggling under **50ms**.
